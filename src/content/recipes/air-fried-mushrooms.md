These are better than mushrooms cooked in a pan.

Seriously good. The air fryer gives them proper heat, concentrates the flavour, and means you are not standing there watching mushrooms give up a puddle of water.

Serve with toast for a fantastic breakfast.

## Ingredients

- 250g mushrooms
- A small amount of oil
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Toast, to serve

## Method

### 1. Heat the air fryer

Set the air fryer to 200°C.

### 2. Chop and season

Chop the mushrooms into a bowl.

Do not slice them too thinly. Quartered or chunky sliced mushrooms survive the blast better and come out tastier.

Add a small amount of oil, salt and plenty of black pepper. Stir so the mushrooms are lightly coated.

### 3. Air fry

Tip the mushrooms into the air fryer basket, trying not to overlap them too much.

Cook for 10 minutes at 200°C, shaking once if you remember.

### 4. Serve

Pile onto hot toast.

Add more black pepper if you like.

## Why this works

Mushrooms need heat and space. The air fryer gives them both, so the moisture cooks off quickly and the edges catch instead of stewing.

## Make it immense

- Add a tiny knob of butter when they come out.
- Finish with chopped parsley.
- Rub the toast with garlic first.
- Add a squeeze of lemon if you want a bit of sharpness.

## No-faff swaps

- Any mushrooms work. Chestnut mushrooms are excellent.
- No olive oil? Any neutral oil is fine.
- Want them richer? Serve with a fried egg on top.
