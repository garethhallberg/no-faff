No mayonnaise. The sesame oil does something mayo never could — nutty, fragrant, a little bit special. Worth saving for when it matters.

## Ingredients

- ¼ cabbage (red, white, or both), chopped small
- 1 carrot, grated
- ½ bell pepper, finely chopped
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- A generous handful of sesame seeds
- Sesame oil
- Salt

## Method

### 1. Chop and grate

Get everything chopped small and combine in a bowl.

### 2. Dress

Drizzle over the sesame oil — enough to coat everything lightly but not pool at the bottom. Add salt and toss well.

Scatter the sesame seeds over and give it one last mix.

## No-faff notes

- Toast the sesame seeds in a dry pan for a minute or two first if you have the time. It makes a difference.
- A splash of soy sauce or a squeeze of lime works very well alongside the sesame oil.
- Both red and white cabbage together looks the part for a special occasion.
