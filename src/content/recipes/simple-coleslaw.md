One spoon of mayonnaise sounds like it will not be enough. It always is. Give it time.

## Ingredients

- ¼ white cabbage, chopped very small
- 1 carrot, grated
- 1 large spring onion, chopped
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped
- 1 large spoon of mayonnaise
- Salt and pepper

## Method

### 1. Chop and grate

Chop the cabbage as small as you can manage — the finer it is, the better it eats. Grate the carrot. Chop the spring onion and chilli.

### 2. Mix

Combine everything in a bowl. Add the mayonnaise and season with salt and pepper.

Stir well. At first it will look like there is not nearly enough mayo. Keep stirring. It will come together.

## No-faff notes

- Good alongside the bean fritters, or tucked into flatbreads.
- Keeps well in the fridge for a day or two — the cabbage softens but the flavour improves.
- Leave the chilli out if you want something milder. Add more if you don't.
