This is what dried beans want to become on a cold day.

Soft onion, garlic, rosemary and enough olive oil to make it feel generous. Crush some of the beans into the broth and it turns creamy without needing cream.

## Ingredients

### For the beans

- 250g dried butter beans or cannellini beans, soaked overnight
- Water, for boiling
- Salt

### For the soup

- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3-4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1 sprig rosemary, or 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
- The cooked beans
- 500-750ml bean cooking liquid
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and black pepper
- A squeeze of lemon

### To serve

- Toasted bread
- Extra olive oil
- Chilli flakes or grated parmesan, optional

## Method

### 1. Cook the beans

Drain the soaked beans and rinse well.

Put them in a large pan with fresh water. Bring to a hard boil for 10 minutes, then lower to a gentle simmer and cook until completely tender and creamy inside.

Depending on the beans, this may take another 45 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes.

Salt towards the end of cooking. Reserve the cooking liquid before draining.

### 2. Soften the onion

Warm a good glug of olive oil in a saucepan.

Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook gently for 8-10 minutes, until soft and sweet.

### 3. Add the garlic and rosemary

Add the garlic and rosemary.

Cook for 1-2 minutes, until the garlic smells good but has not coloured.

### 4. Add the beans

Add the cooked beans and 500ml of the bean cooking liquid.

Season with black pepper and a little salt.

### 5. Simmer

Bring to a gentle simmer and cook for 15-20 minutes.

Stir now and then. Add a little more cooking liquid if it needs loosening.

### 6. Make it creamy

Crush a generous spoonful of beans against the side of the pan and stir them back through the soup.

Keep crushing until it is as thick as you like. Leave plenty whole.

### 7. Finish

Remove the rosemary sprig, if using.

Taste for salt and pepper, then add a small squeeze of lemon to sharpen everything up.

Ladle into bowls and finish with olive oil. Eat with toast.

## Why this works

The cooking liquid gives the broth body, the crushed beans thicken it, and the lemon stops the whole pan feeling beige and heavy.

## No-faff notes

- Rub the toast with a cut clove of garlic while it is hot.
- A pinch of chilli flakes at the end is good.
- Grated parmesan on top makes it richer, if you are not keeping it vegan.
- It thickens as it sits. Loosen leftovers with a splash of water when reheating.

## No-faff swaps

- No butter beans? Cannellini beans are ideal too. Haricot beans also work, but will make a less chunky soup.
- No rosemary? Use a sprig of thyme or a few sage leaves. No herbs at all is fine: lean into the garlic, black pepper and lemon.
- No onion? Use a leek, or just add another clove of garlic.
- Want greens? Stir in spinach or shredded kale for the final few minutes.
- Want it completely smooth? Blend the lot and add a little more stock if needed.
