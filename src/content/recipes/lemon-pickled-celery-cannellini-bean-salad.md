Creamy beans and sharp, crunchy celery. That is basically the whole recipe.

The celery sits in lemon juice while the beans cook, then olive oil turns the lot into a proper dressing. Eat it warm, lukewarm or straight from the fridge.

## Ingredients

- 250g dried cannellini beans, soaked overnight
- 3-4 celery sticks, thinly sliced
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

### Optional extras

- Chopped parsley
- Chilli flakes
- Toasted bread

## Method

### 1. Cook the beans

Drain the soaked beans and rinse well.

Put them in a pan with plenty of fresh water. Bring to a hard boil for 10 minutes, then lower to a gentle simmer.

Cook until completely tender and creamy inside, around 1 hour 20 minutes more.

Salt towards the end of cooking, then drain.

### 2. Pickle the celery

While the beans cook, put the sliced celery in a bowl with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt.

Mix well and leave it to sit. The lemon takes the raw edge off the celery but leaves it crisp.

### 3. Dress the celery

Once the celery has had time in the lemon juice, add the olive oil and plenty of black pepper.

Mix again. Taste and add more salt if it needs it.

### 4. Combine

Add the warm drained beans to the celery and its dressing.

Toss gently, then leave for 10 minutes so the beans drink in some of the lemon and olive oil.

### 5. Serve

Taste once more for salt, pepper and lemon.

Finish with parsley or chilli flakes if you like. Eat as it is, or pile it onto toast.

## Why this works

Cannellini beans are soft and mild. The lemony celery gives them crunch, acidity and something to push against.

## No-faff notes

- This is particularly good while the beans are still a little warm.
- It keeps well in the fridge and is excellent the next day.
- A final drizzle of olive oil before serving is never a bad idea.

## No-faff swaps

- No cannellini beans? Butter beans or haricot beans work well.
- No celery? Use thinly sliced fennel, or cucumber added just before serving.
- No parsley? Leave it out, or use a few chopped celery leaves.
- Want it more substantial? Add seared tofu, walnuts or a slice of good toast.
