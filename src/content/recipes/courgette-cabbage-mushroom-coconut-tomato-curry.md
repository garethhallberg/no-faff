Magnificent and quick.

This is a coconut curry bulked out with cabbage, courgette and mushrooms, then deepened with tomato.

I happened to have leftover [Marcella Hazan-style tomato sauce](/recipes/marcella-hazan-tomato-sauce/) in the fridge. Half a can of chopped tomatoes, or more if you want it saucier, works too.

## Ingredients

- 1 courgette, sliced into half-moons
- 150g cabbage, roughly shredded
- 3 shallots, finely sliced
- 150g mushrooms, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 red chilli, sliced
- 400ml coconut milk
- 125ml leftover tomato sauce, or half a can of chopped tomatoes
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon ground coriander
- 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/2 teaspoon salt

## Method

### 1. Soften the aromatics

Heat the vegetable oil in a large pan or wok over a medium heat.

Add the shallots, garlic and chilli. Cook for about 3 minutes, until fragrant and softened.

### 2. Bloom the spices

Stir in the cumin, coriander and turmeric.

Cook for 30 seconds, stirring constantly, until aromatic. Do not let the spices catch.

### 3. Add the veg

Add the mushrooms, courgette and cabbage.

Stir to coat everything in the spices, then cook for about 4 minutes, until the mushrooms release their liquid and the cabbage starts to soften.

### 4. Simmer

Pour in the coconut milk and tomato sauce or chopped tomatoes.

Add the salt and soy sauce or tamari. Stir well and bring to a gentle simmer.

Cook for about 10 minutes, until the courgette and cabbage are tender and the sauce has come together.

### 5. Finish

Take off the heat and stir in the lime juice.

Taste. Add more lime, salt or chilli if it needs lifting.

Serve with rice.

## Why this works

The coconut milk gives you richness quickly. The tomato brings sweetness and acidity. Leftover sauce adds cooked-down depth, but chopped tomatoes do the job perfectly well.

Cabbage is doing more than bulking it out. It softens into the sauce and makes the curry feel like dinner rather than vegetables floating in coconut milk.

## No-faff notes

- Deseed the chilli if you want less heat.
- If the curry looks too thick, add a splash of water and simmer for another minute.
- If it tastes flat, it probably needs lime or salt, not more spice.
- Leftover tomato sauce with butter makes this richer. Chopped tomatoes make it lighter and sharper.

## No-faff swaps

- No shallots? Use 1 small onion.
- No courgette? Use green beans, broccoli or peppers.
- No mushrooms? Add more cabbage or a handful of chickpeas.
- No leftover tomato sauce? Use half a can of chopped tomatoes, or more if you want a looser curry.
- No lime? Use lemon or a small splash of rice vinegar.
