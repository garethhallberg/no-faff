An omelette is only as good as what goes in before the eggs.

Cook the vegetables properly first. Let the onion soften, let the mushrooms give up their water, let the courgette and cabbage catch a little. Then add the eggs.

## Ingredients

- 1 small onion, finely sliced or chopped
- 1/2 courgette, chopped
- A small handful of white cabbage, finely sliced
- 3 mushrooms, sliced
- 3 eggs
- A little grated cheese
- Oil or butter, for frying
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

### 1. Cook the onion

Heat a little oil or butter in a frying pan over a medium heat.

Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook for about 5 minutes, until it is starting to turn translucent.

### 2. Add the vegetables

Add the courgette, cabbage and mushrooms.

Cook until the vegetables are soft, the mushrooms have released their water, and everything is starting to caramelise a little.

This is where the flavour is. Do not rush it.

### 3. Add the eggs

Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper.

Pour them over the vegetables and tilt the pan so the egg settles evenly.

Cook gently until the omelette is mostly set.

### 4. Finish with cheese

Sprinkle a little grated cheese over the top.

Let it melt, then slide the omelette onto a plate. Fold it if you like. Do not if you do not.

## Why this works

The onion gives sweetness, the mushrooms bring savoury depth, the courgette softens everything, and the cabbage adds a bit of bite. Cooking the veg first means the eggs stay rich instead of turning watery.

## No-faff notes

- Keep the heat moderate once the eggs go in. Scorched egg is not the goal.
- If the top is still a bit loose, put a lid on the pan for a minute.
- A little cheese is enough. You want an omelette, not a cheese blanket.

## No-faff swaps

- No courgette? Use pepper, spinach or leftover cooked potatoes.
- No cabbage? Leave it out, or use kale sliced very finely.
- No mushrooms? Add more onion or courgette.
- Any cheese works: cheddar, Red Leicester, Parmesan, feta-style cheese.
- Want it more substantial? Eat with toast or [lemon rice](/recipes/lemon-rice/).
