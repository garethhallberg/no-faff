Cheap cafe food. A can of beans made glossy and savoury, then piled high on properly toasted bread.

Sun-dried tomatoes do most of the work. Garlic and lemon make sure the beans do not taste worthy.

## Ingredients

- 1 can white beans, such as cannellini, butter beans or haricot beans
- 5-6 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 teaspoon tomato puree, optional
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil, plus more to finish
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- Salt and black pepper
- 2 large slices good bread
- A splash of water

### Optional extras

- Chilli flakes
- Chopped parsley or basil
- Grated Parmesan, Manchego-style cheese or mature cheddar

## Method

### 1. Start the garlic

Warm the olive oil in a frying pan over a medium-low heat.

Add the garlic and cook for about 1 minute, until fragrant but not browned.

### 2. Add the tomatoes

Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes and the tomato puree, if using.

Cook for another minute, stirring everything through the oil.

### 3. Add the beans

Drain the beans, then tip them into the pan with a splash of water.

Season with black pepper and a small pinch of salt. Go carefully: the tomatoes may already be salty.

Cook for 3-5 minutes, until hot and starting to soften.

### 4. Mash a bit

Crush about half the beans with the back of a spoon.

Stir them through the whole beans until everything looks rough, creamy and spoonable. Add another splash of water or olive oil if it looks dry.

### 5. Toast and finish

Toast the bread properly.

Take the beans off the heat and stir through the lemon juice.

Pile onto the toast and finish with more olive oil, black pepper, herbs or chilli flakes if you like.

## Why this works

The mashed beans turn creamy, the whole beans keep some texture, and the sun-dried tomatoes bring concentrated sweetness and savoury depth. Lemon wakes the whole thing up at the end.

## Make it immense

- Rub the hot toast with a cut clove of garlic before spooning on the beans.
- Add grated hard cheese over the top.
- Serve with a sharp green salad or [tomato salad](/recipes/tomato-salad/).
- Scatter over toasted walnuts for crunch.

## No-faff swaps

- No white beans? Chickpeas work, though the texture will be chunkier.
- No sun-dried tomatoes? Use roasted peppers and an extra teaspoon of tomato puree.
- No fresh lemon? A splash of red wine vinegar will sharpen it up.
- No good bread? Put the beans over rice or a baked potato instead.
