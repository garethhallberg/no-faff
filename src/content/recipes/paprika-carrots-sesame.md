This is the way to do carrots. First they stew in butter and honey until the liquid boils off and they take on a glossy sheen. Then you hit them with a creamy paprika sauce and a scatter of sesame seeds before they go into the oven to finish. Sweet, smoky, rich — and the sesame adds just the right amount of toasty crunch.

## Ingredients

- 675g carrots
- 50g butter
- 1 teaspoon clear honey
- 1 clove garlic, very finely chopped
- 10g besan (chickpea) flour
- 1 tablespoon tomato purée
- 275ml milk
- 2 teaspoons warm sweet paprika
- 1 teaspoon sesame seeds

## Method

### 1. Prep the carrots

Preheat the oven to 180°C.

Scrub the carrots. If they are small, leave them whole. If long and thin, cut them into 6cm lengths. If they are wide, halve or quarter them lengthways too.

### 2. Glaze

Put the carrots into a saucepan with 25g of the butter and the honey. Just cover them with water. Cover with a lid and set over a moderate heat for 20 minutes.

After ten minutes, remove the lid. If there is still liquid left, keep cooking uncovered until the carrots are glazed and the liquid has evaporated.

### 3. Start the sauce

Transfer the carrots to a flat, ovenproof dish. Don't wash the saucepan.

Put the remaining 25g of butter in it and set over a low heat. Add the garlic and let it soften but not brown.

### 4. Finish the sauce

Stir in the flour and let the mixture bubble for a minute. Stir in the tomato purée.

Take the pan off the heat and stir in the milk. Add the paprika. Return to the heat and bring to the boil, stirring all the time. Simmer gently for 2 minutes.

### 5. Bake

Pour the sauce over the carrots. Scatter the sesame seeds on top.

Bake for 30 minutes until the sauce is bubbling and the sesame seeds are golden.

## Why this works

The initial glazing concentrates the flavour of the carrots and gives them a head start on tenderness. The paprika in the creamy sauce adds depth and warmth, while the sesame seeds give a nutty crunch that contrasts perfectly with the soft carrots.

## Make it immense

- A pinch of cumin seeds with the sesame for extra warmth.
- Fresh coriander chopped over the top to serve.
- A crumble of feta or goat's cheese for a salty tang.

## No-faff swaps

- Plain flour works if you don't have besan.
- Any milk will do — use what you have.
- No sesame? Try pumpkin seeds or flaked almonds.
