Pasta doesn't get much simpler than this. Or much better.

## Ingredients

- 250g tagliatelle
- 160g mushrooms, thinly chopped
- 1 clove garlic, thinly chopped
- 20 black olives
- 50ml olive oil
- 30g Parmesan, grated
- Extra virgin olive oil, for finishing
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

### 1. Cook the pasta

Bring a pan of well-salted water to the boil. Cook the tagliatelle according to packet instructions.

### 2. Start the sauce

While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over a medium heat.

Add the mushrooms and garlic. Cover with a lid and leave them to soften for a few minutes.

### 3. Add the olives

After a few minutes, add the olives to the pan. Leave everything to cook gently while the pasta finishes. Season with salt and black pepper.

### 4. Combine

When the pasta is al dente, reserve a mug of the cooking water, then drain.

Tip the pasta into the frying pan with the mushrooms and olives. Toss everything together, adding a splash of the pasta water if it needs loosening.

### 5. Finish

Take the pan off the heat. Stir through most of the Parmesan, then drizzle with extra virgin olive oil. Scatter the remaining Parmesan on top.

## No-faff notes

- Any pasta works — spaghetti, linguine, pappardelle. Use what you have.
- No black olives? Green olives, Kalamata, stuffed olives — all fine.
- A pinch of chilli flakes at the end if you want a bit of heat.
- Grated Parmesan or pecorino on top, if you're feeling fancy.
