Lemony lentils taste of summer.

## Ingredients

- 1 can green lentils, drained
- 120g cornichons, roughly chopped
- Juice of ½ lemon
- A good glug of extra virgin olive oil
- Salt

## Method

### 1. Mix

Tip the drained lentils into a bowl. Add the chopped cornichons, lemon juice and olive oil.

### 2. Season

Sprinkle with salt. Mix well.

### 3. Serve

Eat straight away, or leave it for an hour for the flavours to mingle.

## No-faff notes

- Any green lentil works — Puy, regular. From a can or pre-cooked.
- No cornichons? Pickled gherkins or capers work too.
- A handful of chopped parsley or dill at the end adds freshness.
- Good with grilled vegetables, cold meats, or just on toast.
- Makes a complete salad with [tomato salad](/recipes/tomato-salad/), [french potato salad](/recipes/french-potato-salad/), cucumber, cottage cheese and [flatbreads](/recipes/yoghurt-flatbreads/).
