This sounds almost too simple to be worth making. It isn’t.

The onions collapse into the butter, the potatoes go creamy underneath and crisp at the edges, and the whole thing ends up tasting far richer than the ingredient list suggests.

## Ingredients

- 675g red potatoes
- 1 large onion
- 65g butter
- 2 teaspoons chopped rosemary
- Salt
- Black pepper
- Lemon, for finishing

## Method

### 1. Heat the oven

180°C fan.

### 2. Prepare the vegetables

Slice the potatoes into rounds around 4–5mm thick.

Slice the onion into rings.

### 3. Build the base

Put a little of the butter into a roasting dish and let it melt.

Scatter in half the onion rings and all the rosemary.

Season lightly with salt and pepper.

### 4. Layer the potatoes

Arrange the potato slices over the onions, overlapping them slightly.

Do not pack them too deeply — a couple of layers is ideal.

### 5. Coat the remaining onions

Melt the rest of the butter gently in a small pan.

Add the remaining onion rings and stir just long enough to coat them in the butter without properly frying them.

Spoon the onions and butter over the potatoes.

Season again.

### 6. Roast

Roast uncovered for around 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes.

Check after an hour. The potatoes should be golden at the edges, soft underneath, and slightly crisp on top.

If needed, spoon some of the butter and onion juices over the paler potatoes as they cook.

### 7. Finish

Grind over black pepper and give the whole dish a small squeeze of lemon just before serving.

Not enough to make it lemony — just enough to sharpen everything up.

## No-faff notes

- Red potatoes work particularly well because they hold their shape while still becoming creamy underneath.
- Slightly dark onion edges are a good thing here.
- Excellent with roast carrots, grilled fennel, mushrooms or good bread.
