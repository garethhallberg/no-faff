More colour, more going on. The red onion does the work the chilli does elsewhere — a bit of sharpness to cut through the mayo.

## Ingredients

- ¼ red cabbage, chopped small
- ¼ white cabbage, chopped small
- ½ red onion, finely chopped
- ½ yellow pepper, finely chopped
- 1 large spoon of mayonnaise
- Salt and pepper

## Method

### 1. Chop

Get everything chopped small. This is most of the work. The finer the cabbage, the better it eats.

### 2. Mix

Combine in a bowl with the mayonnaise. Season, stir well, and leave it a moment to come together.

## No-faff notes

- The red cabbage will bleed a little into the mayo. That is fine — it turns a pleasant pink.
- Keeps well in the fridge for a couple of days.
