This is barely a recipe and that is the point. Be confident in the ingredients.

## Ingredients

- 2 ripe tomatoes (1 per person, or more if they are small)
- Extra virgin olive oil
- Balsamic vinegar
- Salt

## Method

### 1. Slice

Slice the tomatoes and arrange on a plate.

### 2. Dress

Drizzle with extra virgin olive oil and balsamic vinegar.

### 3. Season

Sprinkle with salt. Seriously this small thing transforms the dish.

## No-faff notes

- Use the best tomatoes you can find. This only works with ripe, flavourful ones.
- Ratio for the dressing: roughly 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar. Or just eyeball it.
- A pinch of sugar in the dressing if your balsamic is particularly sharp.
- Fresh basil leaves if you have them - if you don't then no need to worry but next time go get some.
