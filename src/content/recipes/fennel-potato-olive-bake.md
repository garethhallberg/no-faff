The [roasted fennel and shallots](/recipes/roasted-fennel-shallot/) idea, turned into dinner.

Potatoes soak up the lemony stock, fennel softens into something sweet, and olives keep the whole dish sharp and salty enough to need nothing more than bread or a green salad.

## Ingredients

- 600g potatoes, thinly sliced
- 1 large fennel bulb, thinly sliced, fronds kept if it has them
- 1 onion or 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- A handful of olives
- 2 garlic cloves, finely sliced
- 1 lemon
- 300ml hot vegetable stock
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

### Optional extras

- A few sprigs of thyme
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Crumbled feta-style cheese or goat's cheese, to finish

## Method

### 1. Heat the oven

200°C fan.

### 2. Start the vegetables

Put the sliced potatoes, fennel, onion and garlic in a shallow baking dish.

Add the olives, plenty of black pepper and a little salt. Go lightly with the salt because the olives and stock are already bringing some.

### 3. Add lemon and stock

Grate over a little lemon zest, then squeeze in the juice of half the lemon.

Pour over the olive oil and hot stock. Toss everything together, then press the potatoes and fennel into a fairly even layer.

Slice the remaining lemon half thinly and lay it over the top.

### 4. Bake covered

Cover tightly with foil and bake for 35 minutes.

The steam and stock get the potatoes tender before you ask them to colour.

### 5. Brown the top

Remove the foil and bake for another 20-30 minutes, until the liquid has mostly been absorbed, the potatoes are soft and the top has golden edges.

If using thyme or chilli flakes, scatter them over for the final 10 minutes.

### 6. Finish

Leave it to settle for 5 minutes.

Scatter over the fennel fronds, if you kept them, and add a final squeeze of lemon if it needs lifting.

## Why this works

Stock makes the potatoes soft and savoury rather than dry. Fennel and onion sweeten as they bake. Lemon and olives stop all that softness turning bland.

## Make it immense

- Crumble feta-style cheese or goat's cheese over the top before serving.
- Serve with yoghurt loosened with lemon juice and salt.
- Add a can of drained white beans for the final 15 minutes.
- Eat with [no-faff vegetarian gravy](/recipes/no-faff-vegetarian-gravy/) if you want it firmly in comfort-food territory.

## No-faff swaps

- No fennel? Use leeks, courgettes or more onion. It will be different, but still good.
- No olives? Use capers, or finish with crumbled salty cheese.
- No stock? Hot water with a little soy sauce or miso will do.
- No foil? Use a lidded oven dish, or add a little extra stock and keep an eye on it.
