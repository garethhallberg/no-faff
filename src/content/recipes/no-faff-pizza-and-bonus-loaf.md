This is the house dough: 500g flour, yeast, oil, salt and water. Half becomes two pizzas. The other half becomes a loaf, because if the oven is already on you may as well leave tomorrow-you some bread.

## Ingredients

### For the dough

- 500g strong white bread flour, plus extra for dusting
- 7g fast-action dried yeast
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, or a generous glug
- 300ml warm water

### For the sauce

- 1 good-quality can tomatoes
- A few basil leaves
- A pinch of salt
- A glug of extra virgin olive oil

### For the pizzas

- A handful of black olives, chopped
- A handful of mushrooms, sliced
- Grated mozzarella
- A little Red Leicester or cheddar
- Olive oil, for the trays

## Method

### 1. Make the dough

Put the flour, yeast and salt in a large bowl.

Add the olive oil, then pour in the warm water gradually, mixing until you have a rough dough.

Tip onto a lightly floured surface and knead for about 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic.

### 2. Let it rise

Put the dough back in the bowl, cover it, and leave somewhere warmish for about 1 hour, until puffed up.

It does not need ceremony. It just needs time.

### 3. Split it

Knock the air out of the dough.

Cut it in half.

One half is for bread. Shape it into a small loaf and put it on an oiled tray. Cover loosely while you make the pizzas.

Cut the other half into two pieces for two pizzas.

### 4. Make the sauce

Blitz the tomatoes with the basil, salt and extra virgin olive oil.

That is the sauce. Do not cook it first.

### 5. Shape the pizzas

Heat the oven to 200°C.

Roll or stretch the two pizza dough pieces into rough rounds or rectangles.

Put them on oiled baking trays.

Spread with tomato sauce, then add mozzarella, chopped olives, mushrooms and a small amount of Red Leicester or cheddar.

Go easy on the toppings. A overloaded pizza cooks badly.

### 6. Bake the pizzas

Bake the pizzas until the base is cooked, the cheese is bubbling and the edges are browned.

Depending on your oven and how thick you shaped them, this will take around 15-20 minutes.

### 7. Bake the loaf

Once the pizzas are done, put the loaf in the oven.

Bake for around 30 minutes, until golden and the bottom sounds hollow when tapped.

Leave it to cool for at least a bit before cutting.

## Why this works

The same dough can be pizza or bread. Pizza wants to be stretched thin and topped lightly. Bread wants a bit more time and a calmer bake. Doing both in one session gets dinner and tomorrow's toast from the same bowl.

The sauce stays fresh because it is just good tomatoes, basil, salt and olive oil. The oven does the cooking.

## Make it immense

- Add a little chilli oil after baking.
- Finish the pizzas with fresh basil if you have it.
- Rub the loaf with a little butter or olive oil when it comes out of the oven.
- Serve with [tomato salad](/recipes/tomato-salad/) or [simple coleslaw](/recipes/simple-coleslaw/).

## No-faff swaps

- No strong white flour? Plain flour works, but the dough will be a little softer.
- No basil? Use a pinch of dried oregano, or just tomatoes, salt and olive oil.
- No mozzarella? Cheddar works, but use less because it is saltier and oilier.
- No mushrooms? Leave them off. Olives and cheese are enough.
- Want all pizza and no loaf? Divide the full dough into four pizzas.
