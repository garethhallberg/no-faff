This is the salad you make when the tomatoes are worth eating and you do not want to cook.

Tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, olives. Feta if there is some. If there isn't, it is still lunch.

## Ingredients

- 3-4 ripe tomatoes, cut into chunks
- Half a cucumber, cut into chunks
- Half a small red onion, thinly sliced
- A handful of olives
- Feta, crumbled or cut into slabs, if you have it
- 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tablespoon red wine vinegar or lemon juice
- Salt and black pepper

### Optional extras

- A pinch of dried oregano
- A few parsley leaves

## Method

### 1. Cut the vegetables

Cut the tomatoes and cucumber into proper chunks, not tiny pieces. Slice the red onion thinly.

Put them in a bowl with the olives.

### 2. Dress

Add the olive oil, vinegar or lemon juice, a pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper.

Toss gently. Leave it for 5 minutes if you can. The tomato juice and oil become the dressing.

### 3. Add feta, or don't

Add feta if you have it.

If you don't, taste for salt and add a few more olives if it needs the salty bit.

## No-faff notes

- This is only as good as the tomatoes. Do not make it with sad, cold ones.
- Keep the pieces chunky. A Greek salad should not eat like salsa.
- Bread is useful here. The juice at the bottom of the bowl is the best part.
- If you are using feta, go easy with the salt until you have tasted it.
