This takes longer to read than to make.

## Ingredients

- 1 can haricot beans, drained
- 2 roasted peppers (from a jar is fine)
- Juice of 1 lemon
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- 3–4 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Salt

## Method

### 1. Blend

Put everything in a blender or food processor.

Blend until smooth.

### 2. Season

Taste. Add more salt, lemon or chilli as needed.

### 3. Serve

Drizzle with a little more olive oil and an extra pinch of chilli flakes on top.

## No-faff notes

- Any white bean works — cannellini, butter beans. Use what you have.
- No jarred peppers? Roast a fresh pepper, or use a couple of sun-dried tomatoes.
- Thinner with a splash of water if it's too thick.
- Good with bread, crackers, crudités — or just a spoon.
