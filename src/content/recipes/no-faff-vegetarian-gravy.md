Vegetarian gravy does not need pretending. It needs browned onion, something savoury, something sharp and enough thickness to cling to a roast potato.

This is the quick version. Start with the base, use whichever depth-makers you have, and adjust at the end.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 tablespoon butter or oil
- 1 garlic clove, chopped
- 1 tablespoon plain flour
- 400ml hot vegetable stock
- Salt and black pepper

### For depth

Use one or two:

- A splash of wine
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon Marmite
- 1 teaspoon miso
- 1 teaspoon tomato puree
- A splash of mushroom ketchup
- A small splash of vinegar

### Optional extras

- A little finely chopped carrot, celery or mushroom

## Method

### 1. Build the base

Melt the butter, or warm the oil, in a saucepan.

Add the onion and cook for 8-10 minutes, until soft and starting to go golden.

If using carrot, celery or mushrooms, add them with the onion.

Add the garlic and cook for the final minute.

### 2. Add some depth

If using wine, add a splash now and let it bubble down for a minute.

Stir in your choice of soy sauce, Marmite, miso, tomato puree or mushroom ketchup.

Do not use everything unless you want your gravy to taste like a condiment cupboard.

### 3. Thicken

Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.

Gradually pour in the hot stock, stirring as you go so the gravy stays smooth.

### 4. Simmer

Bubble gently for 5-10 minutes, until the gravy is glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.

Leave it chunky, or strain it if you want a smoother gravy.

### 5. Balance

Taste before serving.

Add salt, soy, Marmite or miso if it tastes flat. Add a splash of vinegar, lemon or wine if it feels too heavy. Add a tiny pinch of sugar if it tastes too sharp.

## No-faff notes

- Too thin? Simmer it down, or stir in a little cornflour mixed with cold water.
- Too thick? Loosen with stock or water.
- Mushroom plus soy sauce makes especially good gravy for sausages or roast potatoes.
- A little Marmite goes a long way. Start cautiously.

## No-faff swaps

- No flour? Mix 1 tablespoon cornflour with 2 tablespoons cold water and stir it into the simmering gravy.
- No stock? Water works if you use soy sauce, Marmite or miso for savouriness.
- No butter? Use olive oil and the gravy is vegan, provided your stock and depth-makers are too.
- No onion? Use a leek or a couple of shallots.
