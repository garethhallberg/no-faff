Make the lentils sharp and cold. Put hot crispy tofu on top. Spoon spicy oil over everything.

That is a proper dinner, and it takes about as long as the tofu takes to brown.

## Ingredients

- 1 batch [lemony green lentils](/recipes/lemony-lentils/)
- 1 block firm tofu
- 1 tablespoon cornflour, optional but useful
- Olive oil, for frying
- Salt and black pepper
- Chilli oil or paprika oil, to finish

### Optional extras

- Chopped parsley or dill
- A spoonful of yoghurt or cottage cheese
- Toasted bread or flatbreads

## Method

### 1. Make the lentils

Make the [lemony green lentils](/recipes/lemony-lentils/) and divide them between two plates or shallow bowls.

They can be freshly mixed or straight from the fridge.

### 2. Prepare the tofu

Pat the tofu properly dry, then tear or cut it into bite-sized pieces.

Season with salt and black pepper. Toss with the cornflour if using.

### 3. Fry until crisp

Heat a good splash of olive oil in a frying pan over a medium-high heat.

Add the tofu in one layer and leave it alone until golden underneath.

Turn and fry until crisp and properly browned on several sides, around 8-10 minutes in total.

### 4. Assemble

Pile the hot tofu on top of the lemony lentils.

Spoon over chilli oil or paprika oil. Add herbs, yoghurt or cottage cheese if you like.

## Bonus: quick chilli oil

Put 1 teaspoon chilli flakes in a small heatproof bowl with a pinch of salt.

Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a small pan until hot but not smoking, then pour it over the chilli flakes.

Leave for a minute, stir, then spoon it over the tofu and lentils.

## Bonus: quick paprika oil

Warm 2 tablespoons olive oil gently in a small pan.

Take it off the heat and stir in 1 teaspoon smoked paprika or sweet paprika, plus a pinch of salt.

Do not fry the paprika hard or it can taste bitter. Spoon over straight away.

## Why this works

The lentils bring lemon and pickle sharpness. The tofu brings hot crunch. The oil connects the two and gives the whole plate a little warmth and richness.

## No-faff notes

- The drier the tofu, the crisper it gets.
- Cornflour is not essential, but it gives the edges more crunch.
- The finishing oils are also good over roast vegetables, rice or yoghurt.

## No-faff swaps

- No tofu? Use fried halloumi, a fried egg or roasted chickpeas.
- No cornflour? Fry the tofu without it and give it enough time to brown.
- No lentils? The same tofu is excellent over the [lemon-pickled celery and cannellini bean salad](/recipes/lemon-pickled-celery-cannellini-bean-salad/).
- No chilli or paprika? Finish with olive oil, black pepper and more lemon.
