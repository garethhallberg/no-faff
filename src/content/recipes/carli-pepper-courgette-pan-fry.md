A quick, punchy side dish with browned courgettes, sweet green peppers and crisp breadcrumbs.

Excellent with potatoes, tarts, gratins or anything vaguely Mediterranean.

## Ingredients

- 1-2 courgettes
- 2-3 Carli green peppers
- 1 echalion shallot
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme, marjoram or herbes de Provence
- 1-2 tablespoons breadcrumbs
- Small squeeze of lemon juice or splash of wine vinegar
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

### 1. Chop the vegetables

Cut the courgettes into chunky half-moons, about 1-1.5cm thick.

Slice the shallot and cut the Carli peppers into roughly 2cm pieces.

No need to deseed skinny Carli peppers unless the seeds look particularly tough.

### 2. Brown the courgettes

Heat the olive oil in a wide frying pan over a medium-high heat.

Add the courgettes and leave them alone for a few minutes, so they get properly browned underneath.

### 3. Add peppers and shallot

Turn the courgettes, then add the peppers and shallot.

Season with salt, black pepper and the herbs.

Cook uncovered for 6-8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the courgettes are golden and the peppers have softened and started to blister.

If the peppers are stubbornly firm, cover the pan for just a minute or two, then remove the lid again.

### 4. Add breadcrumbs

Add the breadcrumbs and cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring, until they have picked up the olive oil and gone crisp.

### 5. Finish

Take off the heat.

Finish with a small squeeze of lemon juice or a splash of wine vinegar.

## Why this works

Courgettes need browning, not apologetic sweating. Letting them sit in the hot pan gives them colour before they soften.

The peppers bring sweetness, the shallot softens into the oil, and the breadcrumbs turn the pan juices into crunchy little bits.

The lemon or vinegar at the end sharpens the whole thing.

## No-faff notes

- Do not overcrowd the pan. Browning is what makes this taste immense.
- Do not put the lid on unless you absolutely need to. Steam is the enemy of colour.
- If using a small pan, brown the courgettes in batches.
- Keep the breadcrumbs moving once they go in, or they can catch.

## No-faff swaps

- No Carli peppers? Use any long green peppers.
- No echalion shallot? Use a small onion or a couple of regular shallots.
- No breadcrumbs? Use chopped nuts, seeds or leave them out.
- No thyme or marjoram? Use oregano, rosemary or mixed dried herbs.
- Want it richer? Finish with grated Parmesan or pecorino, but it will no longer be vegan.
