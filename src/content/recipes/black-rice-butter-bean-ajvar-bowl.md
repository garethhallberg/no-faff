Easy vegetarian food that tastes immense.

Nutty black rice, creamy butter beans, crunchy salad, ajvar, hummus and a handful of nuts and seeds over the top. Very low effort, very good lunch.

## Ingredients

- 150g black rice
- 1 x 400g tin butter beans, drained
- 4-5 sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- 1/2 cucumber, chopped
- 2-3 fresh tomatoes, chopped
- 3-4 tablespoons ajvar
- 3-4 tablespoons hummus
- Small handful walnuts, roughly chopped
- 1-2 tablespoons sunflower seeds
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- A little oil from the sun-dried tomato jar, optional
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

### 1. Cook the rice

Put the black rice in a pan with plenty of cold water.

Bring to the boil, then simmer uncovered for 30-40 minutes, until tender but still pleasantly chewy.

Drain well.

### 2. Mix the beans

While the rice cooks, mix the butter beans with the chopped sun-dried tomatoes.

Add a little oil from the sun-dried tomato jar if you fancy it.

### 3. Assemble

Divide the rice between bowls.

Pile on the butter beans, cucumber and fresh tomatoes.

Add generous spoonfuls of ajvar and hummus.

### 4. Finish

Scatter over the walnuts and sunflower seeds.

Finish with lemon juice, black pepper and a little salt.

## Why this works

Black rice brings chew and nuttiness. Butter beans bring creaminess. Cucumber and fresh tomatoes stop the bowl feeling heavy.

Ajvar, hummus, lemon and tomato oil become the dressing once you start eating. That is why nothing needs dressing separately.

## No-faff notes

- Drain the rice well. Watery rice makes a sad bowl.
- Use generous spoonfuls of ajvar and hummus. They are doing the sauce work.
- The sun-dried tomato oil is optional, but good.
- This is best with contrast: chewy rice, creamy beans, crunchy salad, crunchy nuts.

## No-faff swaps

- No black rice? Use brown rice, red rice, bulgur or couscous.
- No butter beans? Cannellini beans, chickpeas or haricot beans work.
- No ajvar? Use roasted red pepper dip, tapenade or a spoonful of pesto.
- No walnuts? Use almonds, hazelnuts, pumpkin seeds or more sunflower seeds.
- Want it richer? Add feta-style cheese or yoghurt, but it will no longer be vegan.
