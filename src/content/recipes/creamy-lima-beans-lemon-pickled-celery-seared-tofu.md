Somewhere between a warm salad and a proper peasant pot.

The lima beans go soft and creamy, the celery stays crisp and lemony, and the tofu turns the whole thing into dinner.

## Ingredients

### For the beans

- 250g dried lima beans, soaked overnight
- Water, for boiling
- Salt

### For the lemon celery

- 3-4 celery sticks, sliced
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 2-3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

### For the stew

- 2 onions, sliced
- 3-4 cloves garlic, chopped
- Olive oil
- The cooked lima beans
- 1-2 ladles bean cooking liquid

### For the tofu

- 1 block firm tofu
- Olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

### Optional extras

- Chilli flakes
- Walnuts
- Parsley
- Toasted bread

## Method

### 1. Cook the lima beans

Drain the soaked beans and rinse well.

Put them in a pan with fresh water and bring to the boil. Lower to a gentle simmer and cook until completely tender and creamy inside.

Depending on the beans, this may take anywhere from 1 to 2 hours.

Salt towards the end of cooking.

Reserve some of the cooking liquid before draining.

### 2. Pickle the celery

Mix the celery with the lemon juice, olive oil, salt and pepper.

Leave it to sit while everything else cooks. The celery softens slightly but stays crisp and fresh.

### 3. Cook the onions

Heat a generous amount of olive oil in a pan.

Cook the onions slowly over medium-low heat until soft and sweet, around 15-20 minutes.

Add the garlic for the final minute or two.

### 4. Build the stew

Add the cooked lima beans to the onions, along with a ladle or two of bean cooking liquid.

Simmer gently for 15-20 minutes, until glossy and slightly thickened.

Crush a few beans against the side of the pan and stir them back through the sauce to make it creamy.

Taste for salt and pepper.

### 5. Sear the tofu

Pat the tofu dry and cut into chunks or slices.

Season with salt and pepper, then fry in olive oil until deeply golden on both sides.

### 6. Finish

Fold some of the lemon celery through the warm beans and spoon the rest over the top.

Add the tofu.

Finish with black pepper, extra olive oil and chilli flakes if you like.

## No-faff notes

- Good with toasted bread rubbed with garlic.
- Walnuts over the top are excellent.
- Parsley makes it fresher.
- Works lukewarm the next day, more like a bean salad.
- A glass of chilled Greco di Tufo would not be stupid.
