This is the kind of thing that defined vegetarian cooking before vegetarian cooking tried to imitate meat. Earthy beetroot, a rich cheesy sauce, red wine in the pan. The feta on top is the twist — salty and creamy where everything else is deep and savoury.

## Ingredients

- 8 small beetroots
- 25g butter
- 15g flour (besan/chickpea flour works well here — a little extra protein and a slight nuttiness)
- 150ml vegetable stock
- 150ml red wine
- 75g cheddar, grated
- 25g parmesan, grated
- 100g feta

## Method

### 1. Boil the beetroots

Top and tail the beetroots but leave the skins on — they come off much more easily after cooking.

Cover with cold water, bring to the boil, then simmer for around 20 minutes. You want a knife to slide in with a little resistance, not fall through. Drain.

### 2. Cool and peel

Tip the beetroots into an oven dish and leave them until they are cool enough to handle. The skins will slip off with your fingers.

Leave them in the dish — that is where they are staying.

### 3. Make the sauce

In the same pan you boiled the beetroots, melt the butter over a medium heat. Add the flour and stir to combine — cook it for a minute.

Add the vegetable stock and red wine together and stir well. Cook for a couple of minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.

Take off the heat and stir through the cheddar and parmesan until melted.

### 4. Bake

Preheat the oven to 200°C.

Pour the sauce over the beetroots in the dish. Crumble the feta over the top.

Bake for 25 minutes, until bubbling and starting to catch at the edges.

### 5. Serve

Good with bread — homemade if you have it.

## No-faff notes

- Besan flour is widely available and worth keeping in. Plain flour works too.
- The wine does not need to be anything special — just something you would drink.
- Leftovers reheat well. The sauce thickens overnight but loosens again in the oven with a splash of water or stock.
